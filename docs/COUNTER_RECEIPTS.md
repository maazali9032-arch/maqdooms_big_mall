# Counter receipts and payment entry — updated 21 October 2026

Customer receipts contain available business identity, a prominent short recorded bill reference and barcode, the original accounting invoice number in a collapsed screen-only reference disclosure, date, customer identity, purchased items/quantities/rates, saved GST details and payment result. Fabric identifiers are printed under the fabric name. Internal floor/POS identifiers and tailoring UUID references remain in records but are not printed on the customer copy. The accounting invoice number is not replaced or fabricated from a scanner alias.

Both retail-only and stitching receipts show Payment status (Paid, Partially paid or Unpaid), Amount paid and Balance due together. Amount paid is used consistently; amounts are taken from recorded payments/balance. Payment method and actual payment date appear when available. Fabric-only receipts omit delivery and tailoring details. Stitching receipts add garment, assigned delivery date, allocated fabric identifiers/quantities and fabric/stitching sale-summary amounts; finished-item purchases on a mixed bill have their own subtotal. Labels distinguish GST-inclusive versus pre-GST entered prices. Detailed design/instructions remain on the tailor copy. The saved GST final-first/taxable-first and separate/combined preferences remain authoritative. Invoice total is prominent and is not repeated in another footer. Customer GSTIN is printed when present. This supersedes the 20 October retail payment-block omission.

For stitching, one Advance received input controls payment state. Zero/blank means no payment, a positive amount below total means a deposit, and the full total means paid. Balance is automatic and invalid/over-total amounts are rejected. The separate paid/unpaid status selector is removed. The Cash/Card/UPI payment-method selection remains independent.

The price-free workshop sheet follows a structured card: Workshop copy header; work-order/customer-bill references; job barcode and scan caption; Garment details rows for garment, customer, each fabric quantity/reference, factory, assigned tailor, delivery and recorded current status; then Tailoring instructions containing recorded design and notes. No separate measurement/component fields currently exist, so the sheet does not invent them. It excludes prices, GST, deposit, balance and costs. Customer receipts print at 58/80 mm; tailor sheets use explicit 80 mm print styling and a wider screen preview. Synthetic browser previews verify paper-width layout; physical device acceptance is still required.

Extra fabric: scan the tailor job code (or customer bill), scan a physical Thaan, enter metres, confirm. A single job is selected automatically; a multi-job bill requires selecting the receiving garment. Existing permissions, open-job validation, source stock checks and idempotent retries remain. The additional issue follows the same job, updates stock, is available on a reprinted tailor work order and leaves the original customer total/advance/balance unchanged. Additional customer charging is not introduced by this UI correction.

Finished-product transfer notes are optional in the UI, matching the existing live `transfer_finished_products` fallback. The movement still records its system reason, operator, source/destination, time and pieces. Mandatory reasons for corrections or other unrelated workflows are unchanged. No database migration is required by this revision.
